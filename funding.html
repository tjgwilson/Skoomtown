Help Build the Future of Skoomtown

We are raising funds for new communal spaces, tent areas, and other upgrades to help make Skoomtown even better.

One of our current goals is a large bell tent to create a welcoming shared chill space for gathering, resting, and hanging out together.

What we are fundraising for

We would love to add a large bell tent to Skoomtown as a communal space and chill area. Somewhere cosy, useful, and a bit magical for people to gather in throughout the weekend.

We are also looking at other practical improvements to the site over time, including new tent areas and general upgrades that will help the town keep growing.

If you love Skoomtown and all we stand for, committing to just a few pounds a month could really help us keep building something special.

We are trying out crowdfunding as a way to support future improvements and keep investing in the event.

Why it matters

Skoomtown has always been built through community, creativity, effort, and care. Funding helps us improve the shared spaces that make the whole weekend feel more welcoming, comfortable, and memorable.

Even small regular contributions can make a real difference when they build up over time.
