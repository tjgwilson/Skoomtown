How do I RSVP?

To RSVP you need to fill out the RSVP form and pay either the deposit (before June 31st) or the full amount (July onwards).

How do I find the RSVP form?

It’s linked in the WhatsApp group info/description, or you can also ask an organiser, or ask the person who invited you.

How do I join the WhatsApp group?

Ask the person who invited you, or an organiser, to add you.

How can I get updates?

We have an announcements channel in our WhatsApp community. Only admins can post, so there’s no spam! You can turn notifications off for other groups in the community, but we recommend keeping them on for announcements.

How do I pay?

Please see this year’s RSVP form for payment details & how much we’re asking for this year.

What if I can’t afford the deposit or full amount yet?

We can arrange a payment plan in smaller chunks, please message Cai Dare on Facebook or WhatsApp (07742936247)

How do I donate?

Please see the donations page

What do I need to bring?

This is a camping party, so please bring your own;

  • Tents
  • Bedding
  • Food & drinks (BYOB)
  • Cutlery, cups, plates etc
  • Water containers & water
  • Snazzy outfits
  • Memes
Is there running water on site?

Yes, there are flushing loos & showers, however water on the site can sometimes run out, so we ask everyone to bring your own drinking water where possible and to be conservative with your showers.

Is there food?

Communal dinner-time meals (all vegan) cost a little extra, usually £5 per night, thanks to our wonderful in-house Kitchen Wench & Queen of Skoomtown (2025-26), Beth! She has recruited a team of volunteer chefs and helpers to cater the optional communal meals.

How do I get in on communal meals?

We ask you to register your interest on the RSVP form in advance so Beth knows who to contact, she will be in touch closer to the party to confirm once she can let you know what the meals will be.

Are there any glamping options?

Yes, but we give priority to organisers because of all the extra work involved. If you’re interested in being on the waitlist please register your interest with an organiser.

How do I help / volunteer?

The RSVP form has a volunteering section, as well as a rollcall for cabaret acts.

If you want to volunteer to run your own activity or get involved in a more unique way, please contact Beaux (07530275810)
