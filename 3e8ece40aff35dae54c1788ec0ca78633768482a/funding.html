How we budget

Every year we do our best to forecast our spending well in advance so that we can keep the party contribution price reasonable. No one profits, all organisers pay the same contribution as everyone else, and anyone in glamping pays the normal rate for it on top. Any spare funds go directly into next year’s budget, but it’s usually pretty close. This way when you pay your contribution you know that your money is being spent on the year you’re actually there, give or take a few quid that might roll over.

Fundraising Goals

We have some monthly expenses as well as big fundraising goals that we would like to collect donations for separately from the party contribution so that we can keep the party contribution price low.

We welcome one-off donations and monthly donations of any amount, even if it’s just £1 a month to support us in our out-of-party running costs.

Monthly donations will go towards monthly costs first, and any excess will go towards bigger goals.

Our main focuses right now:

  • Mixcloud subscription so we can upload DJ sets for everyone to listen to or download
  • Monthly contribution towards long term storage of set, decorations and equipment
  • Better furniture, contingent on enough funding for adequate storage space first
  • Some very large bell tents
  • A large marquee
