FACTION ANNOUNCEMENT

26 Aug 2026

🚀🚀FACTION ANNOUNCEMENT! 🚀🚀

(If you don't want flavour text, please skip through as there is important info regarding the drinks competition!)

🛰️🧑‍🚀RETURN OF FLIGHT CREW🧑‍🚀🛰️

Calling all Flight Crew! Past, present, future and barely fucking conceptualised. We are the glue that holds this ship together. The engineers, the scientists, the DIY bodge-jobbers and the Google Flight Simulator wizards. Even if your resumé is just a single line talking about how you once fixed a leaky tap at your nan's place, your skills are what we need. Last year we were so busy wrestling with eels in the bowels of the ship come submarine that we didn't even get a chance to party. Well, relegation to the chain locker no more! Flight Crew emerges to once more lead the way with all the bravery and determination of people that are in their eighteenth hour of a twenty hour shift, whose pockets are filled with bits of wire and wago clips and whose palms bear the blisters of manual screwdrivers used to destruction. 🛠️🪛🔧🧰

Our mission has never been more critical. I mean think about it, this damn ship has already undergone three planetary takeoffs and two atmospheric re-entries, our poor baby is in bad shape! Not only that, but mushrooms?! I know they seem like they'd make for a nice squishy landing but trust me, once that mycelial sludge gets into the air intake valves it's gonna be a right bitch to get it out again. I'm talking pipe cleaners, toothpicks, those compressed cans of air people use to clean out the inside of their mid as fuck gaming pcs… God, I really hope we land on a nice bracket fungus and not a motherfucking cep. Those bastards are so wet in the middle… 🍄‍🟫🍄‍🟫

Sorry, got a little off topic there. But y'know, as captain it's my job to worry about this shit so I'm not really all that sorry. Fuck, and that's not even considering all the mushroom related challenges that might arise once we're on the ground! Aluminium eating ergot, machinery melting morels, giant puffballs with legs that just straight up decide to start nesting in the crow's nest. Anything could happen!

Since Flight Crew are going to be working so very hard, and to celebrate our return from the murky bowels of the ship smeared with oil and superglue, it is my honour as captain to announce that we will also be hosting an RnR event for all! (Although of course Flight Crew deserve it more that you layabout passengers.) This year the annual

🍹🍸DRINKS COMPETITION🍸🍹

will be held in the reactor engine room! Please bring cocktails with the theme of dubious mechanical fluids, non-essential ship components and standardised thingamybobs. Can't think of anything? There's always a spare tub of high viscosity, low animosity windscreen wiper lube lying around at the back of the supply closet (now with only 15% cockroach parts!) Bring your best, most beautiful and most outrageous engineering related concoctions for a night of levity alongside the raging (and slightly radioactive) fires that fuel the vibe turbines. See you there at dinnertime on Sunday! Be there, or be a hexagonal safety bolt that no one can find the correct driver to turn.

STEM

Captain Martha, signing off.

Food standards and allergens

10 Aug 2026

A brief message about the food standards we're working to with regards to allergens to ensure we're on the same page about the safety of the food at Skoomtown:

All ingredients will be thoroughly checked, all equipment/surfaces will be cleaned prior to use, and cross contamination procedures will be in place at the festival to mitigate the risk of other people bringing in allergens. The meals should be vegan, gluten and nut free.

However, we cannot guarantee there will be no traces of allergens present in the meals. Due to these meals being made onsite, there will be traces of almost every allergen present and, while we will do our best to reduce the presence of these, we cannot be 100% safe.
